Science Sims

Interactive science simulations for the classroom. No login needed.

  • Circulation LabFollow blood through the heart, lungs and organs, take blood samples, view them under a microscope and see how exercise changes the heart.
  • Roller Coaster Energy LabSet the start height, starting speed, loop and hill, then watch gravitational and kinetic energy change as the coaster tries to make it through the loop and over the hill.
  • Spring DampingDrag a mass on a spring and release it to see Hooke's law and simple harmonic motion, then add friction to watch the motion dampen on a live displacement–time graph.
  • Hooke's Law / Simple Harmonic Motion Virtual LabHang labelled masses on a spring, read the stretch on a magnified metre stick and time 20 oscillations, collecting the same data as the in-class Hooke's law and SHM lab. Good for students who missed the lab.
  • Adding Forces at AnglesWatch two electric forces on a charge get split into x and y components, added tip to tail, and rebuilt into one net force, with Pythagoras for the size and tangent for the direction.
  • Ecosystem Food ChainsPick a boreal forest, Arctic tundra, northern lake, meadow or African savanna. Add or remove living things, start a disease, and watch the whole food chain respond on a graph, an energy pyramid and a data table.
  • Photosynthesis LabShine a lamp on pondweed and watch oxygen bubbles rise into a measuring tube. Change light intensity, carbon dioxide, temperature and light colour, and compare them with the oxygen flow on a simple bar graph.
  • Reaction BenchMix two reactants in an open flask, a gas collection setup or a sealed container on a balance. Use a thermometer, phenol red, glowing and burning splints and a magnifier to find evidence of chemical change, and check whether mass is conserved.
  • Frog Dissection LabDissect a preserved frog step by step or on your own: examine the outside and mouth, open the body cavity, remove and cut open organs, and expose the brain. Explore the skeleton and learn the tissues and organ systems in each structure.
  • Carbon Cycle SimulatorTap the parts of the carbon cycle to see what each one does. Change fossil fuel burning, cutting forests and volcanoes to see if carbon dioxide in the air goes up or down by 2100. Then tap the steps of the nitrogen cycle.
