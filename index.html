Science Sims

Interactive science simulations for the classroom. No login needed.

  • Circulation LabFollow blood through the heart, lungs and organs, take blood samples, view them under a microscope and see how exercise changes the heart.
  • Roller Coaster Energy LabSet the start height, starting speed, loop and hill, then watch gravitational and kinetic energy change as the coaster tries to make it through the loop and over the hill.
  • Spring DampingDrag a mass on a spring and release it to see Hooke's law and simple harmonic motion, then add friction to watch the motion dampen on a live displacement–time graph.
  • Hooke's Law / Simple Harmonic Motion Virtual LabHang labelled masses on a spring, read the stretch on a magnified metre stick and time 20 oscillations, collecting the same data as the in-class Hooke's law and SHM lab. Good for students who missed the lab.
  • Meadow Food ChainChange the grass, grasshopper, frog and heron populations, add disease or try what-if events, then watch the whole food chain respond on a graph, an energy pyramid and a data table.
