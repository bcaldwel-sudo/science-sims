Your circulatory system

The heart is two pumps side by side. The right side sends oxygen-poor blood to the lungs. The left side sends oxygen-rich blood to the rest of the body. Click any chamber, valve, vessel or organ in the diagram to learn what it does.

Blue is a diagram colour for oxygen-poor blood. Real oxygen-poor blood is dark red.
