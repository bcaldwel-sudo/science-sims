How to use this virtual lab

This simulation replaces the equipment from the in-class lab. Use your lab handout to record everything, just like in class.

  1. Choose a spring (top right) and write its number on your handout. Use the same spring for the whole lab.
  2. L₀: with no mass on the spring, read where the tip of the red tape flag points on the magnified metre stick. Read to the nearest 0.1 cm.
  3. Part A: drag masses from the tray onto the spring's hook to build each load in Table A (e.g. 200 + 50). Press Steady the mass, wait until it is still, then record L.
  4. Part B: hang a Table B load and steady it. Press Put tape at flag to mark the rest position. Drag the masses down about 3 cm and let go (or use the 3 cm button).
  5. Start the stopwatch (Space) as the flag passes the tape moving down. Count each later downward pass and stop the watch at 20. Do 3 trials for each load.

Lab rules still apply: at most 3 masses on the spring and never more than 1000 g.
